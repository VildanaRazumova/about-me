## 1. Freeing a busy team's time before asking for their feedback
tags: [persuasion, adoption, stakeholders, change management, automation, Überzeugungskraft]
project: Competitor price monitoring
- **Situation:** The pricing team had to test my pricing system but had no time: collecting competitor prices by hand took up most of their working day.
- **Task:** Free up their time so they could test.
- **Action:** I automated competitor price monitoring: prices are now collected every day through an external data API.
- **Result:** The manual routine is gone, and the team had time to test the pricing system.

## 2. Data and scenarios first, then code
tags: [structured approach, initiative, requirements, Eigeninitiative, Analytisches Denken]
project: Payments assistant
- **Situation:** The finance team kept getting the same questions, like "has this payment arrived?".
- **Task:** Design a bot to answer them.
- **Action:** I first analysed 6 months of chat history, grouped the questions and wrote scenarios. We chose the main one, payment status, and automated it in Microsoft Teams.
- **Result:** Staff now ask the assistant about the payment status of a booking; if there is a problem, it offers to open a Jira ticket routed to the right team. In production since mid-July 2026.

## 3. From banking risk to machine learning
tags: [learning, career change, motivation, Lernbereitschaft, Veränderung]
project: Master's thesis
- **Situation:** 10.5 years at Halyk Bank, up to senior operational risk manager, then project manager and business analyst at Choco Holding.
- **Task:** Move into machine learning and AI.
- **Action:** I did a Master's in Data Science, AI & Digital Business at GISMA, a 6-month AI internship at Linde, and took over the ML pricing project at a tour operator.
- **Result:** My thesis got 96 out of 100, and its pricing recommendation became the basis of my tour pricing work.

## 4. Traveller documents in two days
tags: [deadline, pressure, prioritisation, ownership, Belastbarkeit, Priorisierung]
project: Travel documents
- **Situation:** The business urgently needed a new way to issue travellers' vouchers and tickets.
- **Task:** Launch in two days, with correct data: a wrong flight time on a document is worse than a late one.
- **Action:** A developer built the page and an engineer deployed it. I owned the data: exact flight times, traveller and payment data, and tourist registration codes generated through the state service's API.
- **Result:** Live in two days; thousands of travellers flew on time with all the documents they needed.
- **Reflection:** Speed does not justify skipping version control.

## 5. Winning back a tired team's trust after a handover
tags: [conflict, disagreement, resistance, trust, stakeholders, knowledge transfer, Konfliktfähigkeit, Vertrauen, Wissenstransfer]
project: Tour pricing
- **Situation:** I took over the pricing project after its owner left, and the pricing team was tired of explaining everything again.
- **Task:** Get the knowledge without taking their time, and not lose it again.
- **Action:** I set up a "second brain": decisions, meeting notes and status in one place. The code and my AI assistants work from it, and new people start without me.
- **Result:** The business trusts the project: the knowledge will not leave with one person.
- **Reflection:** People resist sharing when it has cost them before; make it pay off for them.

## 6. Explaining a technical project without being in the room
tags: [communication, explaining to non-technical people, stakeholders, documentation, Kommunikation, Stakeholder-Management]
project: Working method
- **Situation:** I am the only data scientist on several products, and business people need answers when I am away.
- **Task:** Make the projects clear without me.
- **Action:** Project documents live in a shared SharePoint space with "explain it to the business" instructions, so anyone can ask Claude or ChatGPT about a project in plain words.
- **Result:** Stakeholders get the status on their own, without waiting for a meeting.
- **Reflection:** A good explanation should not depend on my availability.
