## Situation and task
The idea comes from her banking background in operational risk. External events, such as fraud, system failures or disasters at other institutions, were analysed only rarely and by hand, which slows the bank's reaction. The task: design a digital solution and its business case.

## What I did
- I mapped how the bank's loss database was built at the time (AS-IS) and a target process (TO-BE) where external news events are collected automatically and classified by AI.
- I drafted the business model, a 10–12 month roadmap, KPIs (for example, processing time per event from about 30 minutes by hand to under 1 minute) and a cost and ROI estimate.

## Result
- A complete concept with a planned ROI; these are planning estimates, not measured results.
- The same idea appears in her financial news sentiment project, where she tested the classification part on real headlines.
